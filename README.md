# Explosion_radiuses
A MineCraft Mod that shows the explosion radius of TNT_MINECARTS, TNT, ANCORS. This mod will help you survive explosions, help you learn the best place to stand, helps remove some of the confusion of Explosions. Unupdated: TNT. ANCORS, CRYSTALS. DO NOT SHOW. only TNT MINECARTS. It takes your armor and difficulty for better radius's.

This Mod takes the difficulty 'hard' 'normal' 'easy' Takes your armor material 'leather' 'chainmail' 'copper' 'iron' 'diamond' 'netherite'
Also takes your enchants 'PROTECTION: 1-4' and 'BLAST_PROTECTION:1-4' Also takes the Enviornment and will show the best place to stand.
And creates A Partical circle to show where you will take the most damage, the least damage, and no damage, 'red: the most damage-close to 

PARTICAL MEANINGS: 'RED: Death or Close to death', 'yellow: less damage-will not die but take significant damage'
'green-you will take very little damage close to 0' 'anything outside- 0 damage'
